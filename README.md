## Mission Overview
Space-themed STEM content often oversimplifies the engineering trade-offs that define a real space mission, or presents them at a level too complex to hold a young learner's attention.

**AstroBase** is an interactive, browser-based space outpost management game designed for students and school-age space enthusiasts. Players take the seat of Flight Director managing either **Moon Base Artemis (Shackleton Crater Rim)** or **Mars Base Ares (Jezero Crater)**.

Players must balance four life-critical, interconnected systems under constant operational tension:
1. **Oxygen & Atmospheric Circulation ($O_2$):** Modeled after NASA's International Space Station ECLSS and Mars MOXIE electrolysis systems.
2. **Electrical Power Grid:** Managing photovoltaic solar arrays, battery banks, and Stirling nuclear fission generators.
3. **Hydroponic Food & Water Production:** Balancing closed-loop water recovery with indoor vertical crop cycles.
4. **Radiation & Thermal Hull Shielding:** Defending the crew against Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs) and micrometeorite impacts using sintered regolith shells and magnetic coils.

---

## Key Features & Educational Gameplay
* **Realistic Space Trade-offs:** Every engineering action has a cost. Boosting oxygen production drains electrical power; harvesting hydroponics consumes water; clearing solar arrays requires astronaut EVA oxygen expenditure.
* **Dynamic Event Engine:** Simulates genuine NASA deep-space operational hazards:
  * Solar Particle Events (SPE / CMEs)
  * Electrostatic Martian Dust Storms
  * Micrometeorite Hull Punctures
  * Cryogenic Shadow Thermal Drops
  * Autonomous CLPS Cargo Resupply Landers
* **Real-Time Animated Radar:** Canvas-rendered orbital scanning radar tracking habitat module blips (ECLSS Dome, Solar Array, Hydroponic Greenhouse, ISRU Landing Pad).
* **Sound Synthesis (Web Audio API):** 100% synthesized cockpit audio, warning sirens, and victory fanfares without external audio files.
* **NASA Tech Dossier:** Interactive educational reference explaining real-world aerospace technologies: ECLSS, MOXIE, Kilopower, and Regolith 3D printing.
* **Zero Dependencies:** Pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript running natively on any browser.
